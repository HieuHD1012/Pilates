# ELLA and Pearl: two additional composition studies

Inspected 3 October 2026 at 1440 and 390 px. Source first-view captures and computed styles are in `source-research/`. These are references for page structure, not sources of photography, copy, identity, pricing or operating facts.

## ELLA Studio · `codex/ref-ella`

Source: [ELLA Studio, Kiel](https://ella-studio.de/).

**Observed:** A compact chocolate navigation bar opens directly onto an architectural reformer-room photograph. A large, calm serif statement sits in the same opening stage. The page then turns to small-group benefits, course choices, introductory offers, self-training and FAQ. The phone version retains a strong room image behind the opening message.

**Adapted hypothesis:** Destination first. A solid cream text panel and a bounded photograph of the *actual Nha Trang room* form one opening spread, with an immediate consultation path. This avoids relying on a washed-out overlay for contrast. Room → why reformer instruction → Group/Private → current schedule → first visit is the page journey. The Services and Studio routes inherit that architectural hierarchy. No ELLA self-training, membership, capacity, price or equipment-quality claim transfers.

**Image roles:**

| Image | Role | Why here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Owner room photograph, small old mark removed | Hero / place proof | Shows the actual reformer arrangement beside an invitation to visit; the text never claims spa architecture the room does not have. |
| Owner reformer action photograph, small old mark removed | Service explanation | Makes the reformer format concrete next to Group/Private choice. Does not imply the pictured person is a trainer. |
| Owner chair movement pair | Instructional sequence | Explains control through two states of one movement, away from the hero. Does not pretend to be a reformer lesson. |

## Pearl Pilates · `codex/ref-pearl`

Source: [Pearl Pilates Nitra](https://www.pearlpilatesnitra.sk/).

**Observed:** A pale, tactile opening with large negative space, a sculptural pearl motif and a restrained navigation. Below are a studio introduction, three class-type cards, team profiles, a three-question selector, testimonials, pricing and contact. The site keeps soft cream surfaces and warm brown calls to action through long pages.

**Adapted hypothesis:** Practice first, with airy cream typography and one full-body real movement photograph in a defined frame. The next chapter shows two available formats as equal choices; a short method section and practical first-visit content replace the source's unsupported three-way quiz and reviews. The Studio and Services routes use spacious editorial composition and tactile cream surfaces. No pearl emblem, Mat/Barre class, trainer identity, parking, testimonial, numerical proof or price transfers.

**Image roles:**

| Image | Role | Why here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Owner reformer practice photograph, small old mark removed | Hero / real practice proof | Shows the body and machine fully; adjacent copy speaks about controlled movement, not a result or named teacher. |
| Owner room photograph, small old mark removed | Place proof | Lets a visitor see the real room before consultation. |
| Owner chair movement pair | Instructional detail | Supports the method chapter; the copy states that this is Pilates movement, not a reformer class. |
