# Image language from a clean `main`

This pass started from `main` at `9d64fb3`, on branch `codex/image-language-from-main`. The previous image experiments were not merged into this branch. The Soul palette and type tokens are unchanged. All six images in `public/images/concept/` are generated layout concepts, not photographs of Soul Nha Trang. Replace them with approved, truthful studio images before launch and recapture every route after replacement.

## Selection, not imitation

Live sites were reviewed at 1440px and 390px on 26 September 2026. Their value is in composition, visual evidence, and conversion flow. Their colors, membership claims, amenities, and scale are not Soul's.

All public routes were captured at 1440px and 390px. The five image-bearing routes were also captured at 1024px and 768px. Across these 30 captures, all image requests resolved and there was no horizontal overflow. Captures show demo data where the development API supplies it; they are visual evidence, not launch-ready business content.

## Image briefs and replacement shots

| Asset                   | Role and replacement brief                                                                                                                                   |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `hero-coaching.webp`    | Wide, two people and the reformer readable; a real instructor helps a real student. Reserve enough light and space for adjacent type, not type on the image. |
| `studio-room.webp`      | Room-wide view with authentic reformer count, circulation and natural light. No people needed.                                                               |
| `group-practice.webp`   | Actual group size, instructor visible, equipment and spatial relationship clear.                                                                             |
| `private-coaching.webp` | One instructor, one student, a visible correction or attentive cue. Obtain consent from both.                                                                |
| `equipment-detail.webp` | A real preparatory act or equipment detail, close enough to see what is being adjusted.                                                                      |
| `arrival.webp`          | Real entrance and what a first-time guest sees. Pair with verified address, hours and contact channels.                                                      |

The images were generated with the built-in `imagegen` tool using six scene briefs: quiet Vietnamese reformer coaching in a warm daylight studio; a compact daylight reformer room; a small group session with instructor; a private coaching correction; hands preparing reformer springs; and an approach through the studio doorway. Prompts requested documentary/editorial photography, natural skin and material texture, no logos, no text, no invented signage, and Soul's restrained warm neutral mood. The generated PNGs were converted to WebP without compositional editing. Every concept alt text says `Ảnh minh họa`; the public footer discloses their status once rather than inserting gallery-style captions under every image.

## Release gate

The concept imagery is for design review only. Before publishing, obtain owner-approved photographs of this branch, verified class size and trainer identities, address/contact/hours, prices and schedule. `npm run check:release` blocks the current concept assets. Replace all generated assets, check subject position and crop again at 1440/1024/768/390, and repeat the full-page visual review. Do not use fictional amenities, people, views, reviews or transformation claims.
