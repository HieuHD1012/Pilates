# Current design direction

J Pilates Nha Trang uses the owner-approved public layout and a warm beige,
copper and brown palette. Preserve the existing composition while maintaining
accessibility and reliable operational workflows.

## Public website

- Editorial Newsreader headings and Be Vietnam Pro text in Vietnamese.
- A shared content grid, hairline separators and deliberate text/image relationships.
- Services and the next action remain readable at phone widths.
- The enquiry creates a call-back lead; copy must not promise an appointment.
- Six temporary concept photographs remain in use until approved studio assets
  replace them. The public footer discloses their status once.

## Private application

- Student/trainer screens emphasize schedules, eligibility and the next task.
- Staff screens use the approved dark navigation rail, bordered paper panels,
  labelled filters, clear record identity and coherent action groups.
- Comparable figures use Newsreader with tabular digits. Status has a label as
  well as colour; pending, failure and retry states are explicit.
