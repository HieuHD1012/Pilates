# ELLA Studio reference variant — structural brief

Branch: `ref/ella-boutique`, created directly from `main` (`9d64fb3`). It does not inherit any layout from the 12 earlier `ref/*` branches or from the parallel `ref/ella` study; it only reuses the owner's already-cleaned studio photographs.

Reference: [ELLA Studio, Kiel](https://ella-studio.de/), observed from the captures saved on `ref/comparison` (`docs/reference-variants/source-research/ella-1440-first.png`, `ella-390-first.png`, full-page slices and `ella.json` computed styles, 3 October 2026). This is a study of a **boutique booking page grammar** — calm card rhythm on a cream canvas held together by a chocolate frame — not a copy of ELLA's words, logo, photographs, offers, prices or facilities.

## What the source does

1. **Chocolate frame.** A solid ~65px chocolate bar (`rgb(99 81 67)`) carries a light wordmark left, plain centred navigation and, on the right, a quiet "Anmelden" text link plus a small white pill "Kurs buchen". The footer repeats the same chocolate field. Everything in between is cream.
2. **Light-veiled room hero.** A full-width warm room photograph; its left ~40% is washed by a cream light veil. On the veil: tiny tracked eyebrow, a three-line condensed serif H1 (72px desktop, 48px phone), one warm paragraph, a solid brown button and an outlined button. On a phone the photograph stays around the text and both buttons go full width.
3. **"Warum ELLA Studio?"** Centred eyebrow + large serif H2, then four soft white rounded cards, each with a round peach icon chip, a serif title and short centred text. On a phone these become a swipe row with dots.
4. **"Finde deinen Kurs."** Two-column grid of white rounded course cards, each with a thin pastel top border, a small pill level tag, a right-aligned level note, serif title, description and a clock + duration footer; a centred dark "Zum Kursplan" button below.
5. **"Dein Einstieg bei ELLA."** Three offer cards; the middle one is filled chocolate with a white button, the right one is outlined with a "recommended" badge. A small outlined "Weitere Angebote" link sits top right. On a phone they become a swipe row centred on the featured card.
6. **Wide white panel.** Photograph left, serif headline right, short paragraph, three hairline-separated spec rows each with a round icon chip, then a pill button.
7. **FAQ.** Centred heading and intro, then a narrow column of soft rounded accordion rows, each with a round icon chip, a serif question and a chevron.
8. **Footer.** Chocolate field, light text, brand line / navigation / contact / social columns, legal row.

The overall feel is warm, calm and boutique: generous whitespace, ~12–16px radii, very soft shadows, chocolate used as structure rather than decoration, and an elegant serif for display.

## What carries across

- The **page grammar and rhythm**: dark frame → veiled photograph hero → benefits row → format cards → start-here offer trio → photo-and-spec panel → FAQ → dark footer. Each chapter opens with a centred eyebrow and large serif heading, and content lives on soft white cards on cream.
- The **dark bar with a light pill** in the header, and the matching footer field.
- **Icon chips** (round peach discs with a line icon) as the repeated tactile mark across benefits, spec rows and FAQ.
- The **featured dark card** among three, used for the one thing a newcomer should do first.
- **Mobile recomposition**: benefits and offers become swipe rows (offers open on the featured card), buttons go full width, the photograph stays part of the first fold.

## Why this is a different hypothesis

The 12 earlier branches are ruled, editorial or poster-like: Tracksmith is a masthead and practice spread, Pillar is architectural symmetry, 1Rebel is hard format tiles, Othership is an enveloping sequence. None of them tests the **soft-card boutique booking page** — the most common grammar among small reformer studios — against Soul's ruled "Measure" system. This branch deliberately does: it lets rounded white cards, icon chips and a dark frame carry the page, so the owner can see whether that familiar, friendly language suits the studio better than the ruled editorial direction. It knowingly departs from the Reference Lock's square structural surfaces; the rounding and the one soft card shadow are variant-scoped in `app/styles/ella.css`, not new global tokens.

## Mobile rule (390)

The first 844px must show the walnut bar, the H1 and the full-width "Đặt lịch tư vấn" button, with the photograph beginning below them. Benefits and offers become horizontal swipe rows with snap points and position dots (offers open on the featured card); format cards and the photo panel stack; FAQ rows stay full width with their chips. No horizontal page overflow at 390/768/1024/1440.

## Honest limits

- Vietnamese is never set in caps (P5), so ELLA's tracked uppercase eyebrows and button labels become sentence-case `label-micro` and sentence-case buttons. The condensed Instrument Serif is not imported; Newsreader carries the display type, which reads wider and softer than the source.
- Rounded cards and a soft card shadow contradict the locked "ruled, square" system. They are scoped to `.el-site` so the experiment can be judged and discarded without touching the token layer.
- Course cards have no duration or level progression because the studio has confirmed neither. The clock footer carries the cancellation window instead.
- The offer trio has no prices. If the owner wants ELLA's price-led comparison, it needs real package data from the backend first.

## Rejected source claims

- "NEW IN KIEL" or any opening/novelty claim; Kiel/German facts, address, e-mail and social handles.
- Self-training with screen instruction (no such product), membership subscriptions, 1/5/10-class cards, trial-class prices and every euro price.
- "Maximal 10 Teilnehmer:innen" and any capacity number (BUSINESS_RULES: capacity is not a brand claim).
- "Eigene Reformer in Premium-Qualität" and any equipment-quality claim; "Erfahrenes Trainerteam" / certified trainer claims; "Stilvolle Boutique-Atmosphäre" as a promise about the room.
- German FAQ facts: what to bring, credit/abo system, waitlist behaviour (Q7 is unanswered), trial lesson existence.
- Vouchers, events and private parties.

## Review after implementation

Screenshots: [`reference-variant-captures/`](reference-variant-captures/). Full pages of Home, Dịch vụ, Studio (`gioi-thieu`), Đặt lịch tư vấn and Lịch tập at 1440, 1024, 768 and 390, plus first folds of each at 1440×900 and 390×844 (`*-first-1440.png`, `*-first-390.png`). They were captured against the MSW dev fixtures, so the schedule rows are labelled demo data. Compared side by side with the ELLA source captures on `ref/comparison` and the clean `main` baseline.

- **What translated:** The walnut frame with a white pill, the veiled-photo hero with a three-line serif H1 and paired buttons, centred eyebrow + serif chapter heads, four icon-chip benefit cards, pastel-topped format cards with pill tags, the three-card start trio with a featured walnut card and an outlined "recommended" card, the white photo-and-spec panel and the soft FAQ accordion all read as the same page grammar as ELLA. Dịch vụ and Studio carry the same cards, chips and a walnut closing card, so moving from Home does not change visual language.
- **Hero contrast:** The text never sits on the photograph. On desktop the image begins 3rem before the copy column ends, and those 3rem are solid cream before the blend reaches the photograph's own bright wall. On a phone the photo is a band below the buttons whose top edge dissolves into cream. No dark scrim is used.
- **Mobile (390):** The first fold shows the walnut bar, eyebrow, the full H1, the paragraph and both full-width buttons, with the photograph starting under them. Benefits and principles become swipe rows with position dots; the start trio becomes a swipe row that opens on the featured consultation card. The mobile menu opens as a cream sheet (checked by script). Captures and a scripted check found no horizontal overflow at 390, 768, 1024 or 1440.
- **Fixes made after looking at the captures:** at 1024 the city line beside the wordmark collided with the centred nav, so it is hidden between 64rem and 80rem and the nav gap tightens; the H1 broke into four lines at 1024 until the copy column widened to 43vw; at 768 the photo panel and the week panel were cramped side by side and now stack until 64rem; the benefits H2 broke mid-phrase and now breaks after the first sentence; the desktop room photo was cropped square so it no longer towers over its copy.
- **What was rejected:** prices, memberships, card bundles, self-training, "NEW IN KIEL", capacity numbers, equipment-quality and trainer-credential claims, the German FAQ facts, ELLA's arched-mirror room photography and its uppercase type. Course duration and levels are not shown because the studio has not confirmed them.
- **What still looks weaker than the source:** The owner's reformer photograph is cool, daylight-white and slightly busy (wall camera, cable, cadillac frame at the right edge); ELLA's hero is a warm, styled room. The blend works, but the photograph cannot deliver the same "Wohlfühlort" glow without a new shoot in warmer light. The room photograph is honest but fluorescent-lit and equipment-dense; inside a white card it reads better than as a hero, but still more "equipment list" than boutique. Newsreader is wider and less fashionable than ELLA's condensed Instrument Serif, so the H1 feels heavier. The offer trio, without prices, carries figures (4 giờ, 4 bước, 8 giờ) that are true but less decisive than a price; it will be stronger once `/goi-tap` has real packages. The footer shows five "Đang cập nhật" slots, which is honest and visibly unfinished.
- **Functional routes:** Lịch tập, Đặt lịch tư vấn, Gói tập, Huấn luyện viên, Liên hệ and Khuyến mãi keep their original layouts, query states and form behaviour inside the new frame; only their inputs and submit buttons take the softer radius. They therefore look plainer than Home/Dịch vụ/Studio, which is a visible seam.
- **Header ask:** On Home and Đặt lịch tư vấn the white pill is "Đăng nhập" because the consultation ask is already in the page; elsewhere it is "Đặt lịch tư vấn" with "Đăng nhập" as a text link. This keeps the source's pill without violating P2.
- **Customer/owner judgment:** This is the most conventional, "boutique studio template" direction in the set, and probably the easiest for a first-time visitor to scan: what it is, why, which format, how to start, answers. It is a credible comparison candidate for the owner's beige preference. It is **not** ready to send as a finished commercial site: address, phone, hours, Zalo/map links, real package prices and approval of the old-mark cleanup are still missing, the demo schedule needs a live backend, and the J Pilates / Soul naming is unresolved.

`npm run verify` passed (typecheck, lint, 66 tests, production build with nine pre-rendered public routes, build contract, content gate code half). Note: the local Node is v22.12.0, which cannot import the `.ts` content files used by `check:build-contract`; verify was run with `NODE_OPTIONS=--experimental-strip-types`. The content gate still lists five unowned launch facts, which no code change can supply.

## Soul palette

This variant applies the owner-approved Soul Đà Nẵng colour roles directly in `app/styles/app.css` (the same values as `apply-soul-theme.mjs` on `ref/comparison`): sand/cream `#fff5ec`, sand-deep/peach `#fce5d1`, chalk `#fafaf8`, ink/walnut `#2c2319`, ink-deep `#1a1410`, ink-2 `#5a4b40`, ink-3 `#806f61`, rule `#e8e5e0`, rule-2 `#d4c5b6`, rule-dark `#6b4b39`, lacquer `#9a4e2d`, lacquer-2 `#7c391f`, lacquer-wash `#fce5d1`, plus cream, peach, copper `#c97b4b`, amber `#d4a574` and walnut aliases; `theme-color` is `#fff5ec`. ELLA's own milk-chocolate `rgb(99 81 67)` is not imported: the frame uses Soul walnut `#2c2319`, which is darker and gives the header more weight than the source. Copper `#c97b4b` and amber appear only as decorative marks (card top borders, footer icons, the active-nav rule); small action text and icons on light surfaces use `#9a4e2d` or darker. Be Vietnam Pro and Newsreader remain the fonts. This is a palette and composition study, not a brand or name transfer from the Đà Nẵng studio.
