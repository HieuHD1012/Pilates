# SATISFY Foundations — detail in motion experiment

Branch `codex/ref-satisfy`, independently cut from `main` `9d64fb3`.

Official [SATISFY Foundations](https://satisfyrunning.com/pages/foundations), reviewed 2026-09-27, begins with a permanent practice story and alternates wide movement imagery with close views of materials. The useful principle is **a detail earns visual weight when it explains performance in use**. Soul sells coaching, not fabric, shoes or running technology; do not copy technical specifications, prices, product grids or athletic claims.

## Structure before implementation

```text
OPEN       One wide working-body frame and one tighter posture frame within a shared stage
DETAIL     A close view of a real reformer element paired with a short teaching point
PRACTICE   The wider room reveals how the apparatus sits in the studio
CHOICE     Group / Private with direct schedule and enquiry actions
ACTION     Live schedule and first visit
```

This differs from Tracksmith's long practice essay: the alternation is *physical scale* (body → apparatus detail → room), not successive editorial chapters. A close crop should not become a decorative texture. A viewer must be able to tell what it shows. The owner archive supplied the opening practice and room frames, but its crops did not show the reformer mechanism clearly. The detail chapter therefore uses a temporary concept asset from `codex/image-language-from-main`, based on the earlier documentary brief for hands preparing reformer springs. It is marked “Hình minh họa” in the UI and blocked by the release gate. A CSS crop does not turn a chair into a reformer or prove a trainer identity. On mobile, the body still reads in the opening and the detail crop stays legible beside its sentence rather than turning into an anonymous strip.

Image roles: real reformer action = practice evidence; real chair sequence = body control, with no reformer claim attached; generated spring detail = conceptual teaching close-up, not evidence of this studio's equipment; real room/equipment = physical setting. The only visible image label is the minimal disclosure on the concept. Release remains blocked by unconfirmed business facts and replacement of that concept with an approved real frame.

## Review after implementation

Captured the Home, Services, Studio and Booking routes at 1440, 1024, 768 and 390 px, including first folds at desktop and mobile (24 files in `reference-variant-captures`). A scripted pass through all 16 route/viewport combinations found no horizontal overflow, broken visible images or hidden mobile menu. The body → detail → room sequence is the clearest distinction from the other editorial candidates; at 390 px it reads as a vertical sequence without turning the detail into a narrow decorative strip. The generated detail remains a material release limitation. Keep this pattern only if the owner can photograph a real reformer detail with equal clarity. Otherwise use the real practice/room frames and shorten the sequence.
