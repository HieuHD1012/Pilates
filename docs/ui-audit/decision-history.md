## Contradictions and lineage risk

`REFERENCE_LOCK.md` already softened “no containers” after its own evidence showed an offer/location/first-step unit needed a boundary. The later `UI_PATTERNS.md` still says “never a card”; the code followed the stricter reading. The same lock says the typography/image balance must be revisited when real images arrive, but the photography branch largely optimized asset treatment and source sizes. The `soul-doi-chieu.html` review used five principles chosen by the Soul-1 team to judge Soul-1; its concrete counter-observations are more transferable than its overall winner statement. The photo report correctly corrects earlier false claims, including an assertion that the old gallery's lazy loading was inconsistent; this demonstrates why repeated agent assertions are not independent evidence.
