# Cross-category visual references for Soul

Reviewed 26 September 2026. This extends [the per-screen workflow](screen-workflow.md) beyond Pilates sites. The references below are live sites inspected at 1440px and 390px; the notes describe what was visible during that review, not an endorsement of every screen or claim. Sites and campaigns can change. Use their composition logic, then photograph and describe **the actual Nha Trang studio**.

## What the examples actually teach

1. **Show a real activity in a real place.** BLOK’s movement frame and Surrenne’s interior are different answers to the same question: “What will I experience there?” Soul needs both answers, at different moments. A reformer image must show a reformer; a room claim needs a room photograph.
2. **Give each photograph one job.** Hero = emotional anchor and service recognition; room = arrival and trust; coach/client = personal attention; class = format and scale. A decorative equipment close-up does not replace any of these. One meaningful image per major decision is enough.
3. **Make atmosphere and choice coexist.** The homepage can be emotional, but a newcomer must reach group/private differences, a real timetable or a contact path without decoding the design. BLOK and Third Space give the visual invitation and navigation together; Barry's supplies the practical answer deeper down.
4. **Premium comes from specificity and restraint.** Architecture, natural light, calm framing, accurate class details and a clear next action carry more trust than black/gold styling or expansive promises. The sites with the loudest visual effects also displayed the most mobile interruptions in this review.
5. **Design the mobile crop separately.** Do not merely shrink a desktop photograph. Check face, hands, machine, coach and text at 390px; preserve the intended relationship between subject and copy. Test the page with video paused, unloaded and with any consent layer visible.
6. **Make the first visit feel known.** State only confirmed details: how to choose a class, what happens on arrival, what to bring, where to go, and what the enquiry/booking action does. The high-end feeling should continue into schedule and forms, where reliability matters more than cinematic layout.

## Soul photography brief, before any new image placement

Request one coordinated shoot or select existing **verified Soul** images in this order:

| Priority | Frame | Job | Crop test |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Instructor guiding a client on a visible **reformer**, with room context | Hero candidate: service + human attention in one frame | 1440 and 390: both people and reformer readable; no face/hand covered by type. |
| 2 | Wide, honest view from the entrance or corner showing the actual training room | Establish the destination on `/gioi-thieu` and `/lien-he` | Geometry and lighting remain truthful; no hidden or invented facilities. |
| 3 | Group session with visible class size and instructor position | Prove how group training differs from a private session | At 390px the group is still visibly a group, not anonymous cropped limbs. |
| 4 | Private correction or coaching interaction | Explain why a private session is different | Consent obtained; the focus is attention and technique, not a staged “perfect body”. |
| 5 | Verified instructor portraits in the same light/background family | Put real people behind `/huan-luyen-vien` | Faces consistent and identifiable in square and portrait crops. |

Choose one light temperature, contrast range and crop language for the set. Use the actual people, equipment and premises only with permission. If any priority image is unavailable, use a text-first composition for that section; do not substitute unrelated stock imagery or add an on-page caption to justify a mismatch. `alt` text still describes meaningful images for accessibility.

## Visual gate for this direction

For each candidate, capture the first viewport and full page at 1440, 1024, 768 and 390px. Review the sequence, not isolated components. Reject when the equipment conflicts with the copy, a photo has no answer to “why here?”, the image and text use unrelated axes, repeated sections resemble a gallery of stock photos, or the mobile crop removes the subject. Reject when video loading leaves a blank primary frame, a fixed CTA hides content, or a consent/promo layer takes over the mobile viewport. Finally ask the owner to verify each factual sentence and image identity before treating a polished screenshot as a launch-ready site.

This is a reference and shooting brief. It does **not** license reuse of reference-site photographs, claims, testimonials, pricing or facility descriptions.
